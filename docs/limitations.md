# Limitations

- The current increment has not executed a full real-EEG feature extraction or produced empirical workload results.
- The dataset has only 18 released participants, limiting generalization and precision.
- The task contains possible tutorial, response, and acquisition-dropout effects that require explicit filtering decisions.
- Stable tie-breaking is deterministic but can affect entropy estimates for quantized or repeated signals.
- The spectral estimator is a compact baseline, not a final EEG preprocessing standard.
- No clinical or diagnostic interpretation is intended.
- Participant-grouped statistical and ML analyses are not yet implemented in this increment.
